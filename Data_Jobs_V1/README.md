# Data Jobs Dashboard w/ PowerBi

![Dashboard page 1](/Image/project%20page%201.JPG)
## Introduction
This dashboard was created for **Job Seekers,Job Transitioners, and Job Swappers** to solve the common problem: information about the data job market is scattered and hard to grasp. Using a *real-world dataset of 2024 data science job posting* (including titles, salaries and locations) this project provides a single, easy-to-use interface to exploer market trends and compestion.

## Skills

- **Data Transformation (ETL) with Power Query:** Cleaned, shaped and prepared the raw data for analysis by handling blanks, changing data types and creating new columns.
- **Implicit Measures:** Formulated measures to derive key insights and KPIs like `Median Yearly Salary` and `Job Count`
- **Core Charts:** Utilized **Column, Bar, Line, Area Charts** to compare job counts and track trends over time
- **Geospatial Analysis:** Laveraged **Map Charts** to visualize the global distribution of jobs.
- **KPI Indicators & Tables:** Used **cards** to display key metrics and **Tables** to provide granular, sortable data.
- **Dashboard Design:** Designed an intutive and visually appealing layout, exploring both common and uncommon charts types to best tell the data story.
- **Interactive Reporting:**
    - **Slicers:** to dynamically filter the report by Job Title
    - **Buttons &Bookmarks:** To create semless nevigation experience.
    - **Drill-Trhough:** To nevigate from a highlevel summery to a contextual, detailed view.

    ## Dashboard Overview
    ### Page 1: High-Level Market View

    This is your mission control for the data job market. It showcases KPIs like total job count, median salary and top job title to give you a quick understanding of what's happening in the job market at a glance.

    ![Dashboard page 1](/Image/project%20page%202.JPG)
